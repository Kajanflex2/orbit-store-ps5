# Orbit Store 1.3.5 (Beta)

**Archive.org sign-in comes to Orbit.**

- **Archive.org sign-in.** Connect your account in the native PS5 app, browser or Orbit Zero to access downloads that require signing in.
- **225 unavailable Archive.org links removed, and 3 games also had to go.** Links that require an Archive.org account are still included.

## Orbit Store · Native PS5 app (FFPKG) and browser (ELF)

- **Smoother Vikingfile and AkiraBox downloads.** Complete the provider’s verification, then wait. Orbit automatically captures the download link and returns you to the app, without manually pressing Download.

## Orbit Zero · Mac, Windows and Linux

- **TorBox downloads on your computer.** Connect TorBox in Download settings, download through supported sources and transfer to your PS5.
- **Recover completed downloads from your computer cache.** Restore a removed download entry and transfer the saved file without downloading it again.
- **Reconnect after your PS5's address changes.** Update its address and verify the connection. Recovery is also available after pairing has been reset.
- **Better download and transfer recovery.** Interrupted computer downloads retry automatically. Resume stalled PS5 transfers or remove stuck entries from Downloads while keeping their files.
- **Clearer errors.** See which stage needs attention and open the original source page directly.
